01 / CONTENT-DRIVEN WEBSITE

Divine Design 144

A distinct visual identity. Content that responds to a simple set of rules.

The design challenge
Bring weekly astrology readings, a guidance library, and zodiac cards into one cohesive page, with less routine content selection.
What Flex IT built
A custom HTML, CSS, and JavaScript website with a night-sky palette, a CSS mandala, and a card layout that stacks on smaller screens.
How the content works
The browser selects a stored reading by calendar week. Guidance comes from a curated library, with a refresh every five minutes while the page is open and auto-mode is on. Visitors can also refresh it manually.
Why that pattern matters
The same idea—approved content plus a defined rule—can help a business rotate announcements or feature the right service without choosing each message by hand.

This earlier project demonstrates the type of dynamic functionality now offered through ACTIAS LUNA™. It predates the brand; its current implementation uses browser-based rules, not AI generation or a server-side daily publishing job.

BEFORE WE START

A few practical questions.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. Share the website and what you want to improve. Tom will review the platform and requested changes with you, then confirm scope and pricing.

Who owns the domain?

Your domain is registered in your name. Domain connection is included in standard builds; registration and renewal fees are separate.

Do I need a monthly plan?

Monthly managed plans are optional. We can discuss technical care, automation, or a self-managed website after launch.

Does automation mean AI?

No. Many useful automations use a schedule or simple rule. We choose the appropriate approach for the task and agree on any external services or costs first.
